You pay only after the repair is complete and you've inspected and approved it. We never charge upfront.

We use OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) parts wherever possible. If we use a compatible part, we'll tell you the grade and price difference before installation. You can choose.
The part installed and the labour work is covered. If the same problem reoccurs within 30 days due to the repair itself, we fix it for free. Physical damage after the repair is not covered.
In most cities, same-day or next-day slots are available. You can book a slot as early as 9 AM or as late as 8 PM.
